# Operations Efficiency Improvement for a Regional Food Delivery Company

**Context & Affiliation**: Strategy Engagement, IIT Roorkee  
**Domain**: Operations Consulting, Logistics Optimization, Unit Economics & Process Re-engineering  
**Focus Subject**: QuickBasket Delivery Services (Regional on-demand food delivery operator across 5 Indian cities).

---

## 1. Problem Statement
QuickBasket faced operational pressure and declining customer satisfaction ratings due to extended delivery turnaround times, suboptimal courier fleet dispatching, and frequent restaurant dispatch bottlenecks across its 5-city network.

---

## 2. Diagnostic & Problem-Solving Approach
- **Operational Diagnostic Audit**: Analyzed over 50,000 order lifecycle timestamps, tracking dwell times across four core segments: customer order placement, restaurant kitchen preparation, rider transit-to-merchant, and last-mile customer drop-off.
- **Root-Cause Identification**: Identified that average courier utilization stood at 61% (vs. 75%+ industry benchmark) due to single-order dispatching, while 22% of orders suffered from inaccurate restaurant kitchen preparation estimates.
- **Smart Dispatch & Batching Engine**: Conceptualized an algorithmic dispatch model featuring dynamic proximity radius matching, multi-order batching along common delivery corridors, and predictive merchant prep-time buffers.
- **Implementation Roadmap**: Formulated a phased 12-month implementation roadmap spanning telemetry integration, dispatch automation, and partner restaurant SLA governance.

---

## 3. Quantified Operational & Financial Impact
- **28% Delivery Turnaround Reduction**: Projected reduction in average delivery turnaround from 42 minutes down to 30 minutes.
- **₹1.80 Crore Projected Annual Cost Savings**: Estimated 15% reduction in operating expenditures through optimized rider routing and reduced deadhead transit.
- **Productivity Uplift**: Projected courier fleet utilization increase from 61% to 76%+.
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Targeted CSAT improvement from 78% to 90% via predictability improvements.
